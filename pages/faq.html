Frequently Asked Questions

Is the result scientific?

No. Animal Totem is designed for reflection and entertainment. Results are symbolic and not based on scientific scoring.

How are results generated?

We use a curated set of animals and associated traits. The generator randomly selects a combination to create a unique profile and image.

Can I regenerate my totem?

Yes. You can generate as many totems as you want and choose a different color theme each time.

Is my data stored?

No. Your input and generated result stay on your device. The app does not save your totem history on a backend server.

Can I share my image?

Yes. The generated image can be downloaded and shared on social platforms such as Instagram or TikTok.
